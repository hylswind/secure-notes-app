All notes, users, the session key and the TLS certificate live on an extra EBS volume encrypted with LUKS. Its key is sealed to this instance's NitroTPM. This means:

  • The volume unlocks automatically after a reboot of the same instance.
  • It cannot be opened on another instance.
  • Stopping and starting the instance (as opposed to rebooting it) discards the TPM storage hierarchy and makes the data unrecoverable.
  • An image rebuilt with different measurements cannot open an existing volume.
